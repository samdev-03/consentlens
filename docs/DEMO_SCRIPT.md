# Human demo: approximately 2 minutes 35 seconds

Record your own screen and voice. Do not use an AI-generated presenter or voiceover. Keep the final video under three minutes. Upload as an unlisted YouTube video and verify access without your account.

Before recording: open the live app, refresh, leave the default research example selected, and close unrelated tabs/notifications. Rehearse once. Use only fictional data. Understand the code and limitations before presenting the entry.

## 0:00–0:20 · The problem

**Show:** The two documents before comparison.

“A consent form can be carefully written, then lose its meaning in a translation or simplified explanation. One changed word can turn a choice into a requirement. ConsentLens makes those changes visible so a person can review them.”

## 0:20–1:00 · A real comparison

**Click:** Compare documents, then the first and second findings.

“This is a fictional English consent and Spanish explanation. The comparison produces four high-priority signals. Here, optional became mandatory. The exact original and explanation appear together. Here, retention changed from thirty days to ninety. These findings come from the editable inputs, not a prerecorded response.”

**Click:** Withdrawal and sharing findings.

“The withdrawal clause was not located, so the app asks me to check the complete text before calling it an omission. And this sharing passage lost its prohibition. Each result stays connected to the words that triggered it.”

## 1:00–1:30 · Human review and export

**Click:** First finding. Type “Restore explicit optional wording before use.” Click Confirm concern, then Export report. Show the preview and close it before continuing.

“I make the review decision and add context. The report includes both complete documents, findings, notes, the engine version, and hashes of the analyzed text. Those hashes identify text; they are not a digital signature. Editing an input pauses export until I compare again.”

## 1:30–2:00 · Honest evaluation

**Click:** Evaluation → Run evaluation.

“The evaluation runs sixteen synthetic cases in this browser, including faithful controls and unfamiliar wording that needs manual review. These are inspectable regression checks. Sixteen passing cases is not a claim of real-world accuracy. The next step is independent bilingual review on unseen documents.”

## 2:00–2:35 · Architecture and limits

**Click:** How it works; show privacy and limitations.

“The engine uses a small English-Spanish vocabulary, clause pairing, and deterministic checks for six types of changes. Documents and text-based PDFs are processed locally, with no model API key or document database. ChatGPT and Codex assisted development. The tool does not certify consent, translation quality, or legal compliance. It makes a focused review more inspectable: exact evidence, explicit uncertainty, and a human decision.”
